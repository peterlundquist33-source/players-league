Absolute 0–100 score (not curved) — roster quality vs fixed positional benchmarks on a 5-source consensus (ESPN, ESPN ADP, FantasyPros ECR, FantasyCalc, Sleeper), plus a draft-value adjustment · 2026-08-29

Five drafts into this thing and the cabin has never produced a board this clean — nobody walked away from Players Weekend with a roster they had to apologize for. Adam did Adam things, turning the Uncut Cokerr into the only A on the sheet, with Peter, Christian and Noah right behind him at A- and each one built on a completely different premise. The tight end run in the fifth and sixth was the one genuine stampede of the weekend, and the guys who sat it out came away with backfields deep enough to make it look intentional. From Logan's B+ down to Leif's C+, this is less a ranking than a list of teams that are going to be annoying to play in November.

Adam Built A Wideout Room And Waited On Quarterback

The plan was obvious in hindsight, which is how you know it was good.
