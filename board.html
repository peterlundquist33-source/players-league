The rules

Scoring

  • Confidence points. Every matchup you call right pays the rank you put on it, 1 through 6. Each rank gets used exactly once, so a perfect slate is 21.
  • The tiebreaker. One real NFL player is featured each week, drawn at random from the season's top full-PPR scorers — never one on bye, never one ruled out. Guess his full-PPR total to a tenth. Closest guess takes +3, so a perfect week is 24.
  • Full PPR means full PPR — 1 per catch, 0.1 per rushing/receiving yard, 6 per TD, 0.04 per passing yard, 4 per passing TD, −2 per interception, −2 per fumble lost, 2 per two-point conversion — computed from his actual stat line, not from anyone's league settings.
  • Closest also breaks ties. Level on points? Whoever was nearer the featured player's total ranks ahead, in the week and in the standings.
  • Ties pay everyone. If two of you are exactly as close, you both get the bonus.
  • If the featured player doesn't play, the bonus is void — a late scratch or a game spent on the bench pays nobody, and this page will say so.
  • A tied fantasy matchup pays nobody — there's no winner to have called.
  • One card per owner per week. Resubmit as many times as you like before the lock; the last one counts. After the lock nothing can be written.
