The rules

Scoring

  • Confidence points. Every matchup you call right pays the rank you put on it, 1 through 6. Each rank gets used exactly once, so a perfect week is 21.
  • Highest score bonus. Closest guess to the week's single best team score: +3.
  • Smallest margin bonus. Closest guess to the week's narrowest margin of victory: +3.
  • Ties pay everyone. If two of you are equally close on a bonus, you both get it.
  • A tied fantasy matchup pays nobody — there's no winner to have called.
  • One card per owner per week. Resubmit as many times as you like before the lock; the last one counts. After the lock nothing can be written.
